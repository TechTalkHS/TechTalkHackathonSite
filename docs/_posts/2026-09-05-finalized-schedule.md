We have secured a location for the hackathon and have finalized the schedule, we will meet at [Southeast Regional Library's](https://maps.google.com/?q=908+7th+Ave.+27529) adult program room for a few days towards the end of the hackathon.

**Notice**
In order to focus the scope of our hackathon we are now doing three guest speakers, they will speak on health care, finance, and public safety.

# Here is the finalized schedule for the hackathon:

## Sunday, September 13th
- We will start the hackathon online, after registration you will be invited to join our Discord server where we will communicate most of the information during the hackathon, we will be able to provide feedback and assistance most evenings during the week.
- We will release a bunch of ideas to help kickstart your brainstorming, and also provide Voice Channels to communicate with your team or with one of us.

## Saturday, September 19th (12:15pm-4:15pm)
- Everyone will meetup in Southeast Regional Library, where we will provide time to work with your team to finalize your project and make sure it will be finished in 2 days.
- We will have guest speakers in each of our sectors, who will talk about the issues in tech and can provide feedback on what you have built so far.

## Monday, September 21st (9:30am-1:30pm)
- We will meet in the Southeast Regional Library, where the teams will submit their projects, and present what they have done. We will then judge the projects and give out the awards.
- If you are unable to pick up your award on this day, please contact us through Discord, our email, or our Instagram.

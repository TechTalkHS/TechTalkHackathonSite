Here are all the details for the first Tech Talk Hackathon.

**The Theme is...**

![Hack For Good]({{ "/assets/img/hackForGood.png" | relative_url }})


This means that every project will be working to solve real problems, they will be judged on their usefullness.

**Details**

This event if made for *High school students* who can participate in *teams of up to 4 people*. People of all expirience levels are encouraged to participate.

The Hackathon will be taking place on September 19th and 20th -- starting at 9:00 AM and ending at 3:30 PM.

There will be 5 guest speakers, all talking on different topics, and how technology can make an impact in different ways. The topics are *Healthcare & Wellness, Environmental Sustainability, Business & Finance, Education, and Public Safety*
